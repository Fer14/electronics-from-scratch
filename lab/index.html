THE LAB

Put the right part in the right slot

Every task is a small circuit with empty slots. Drop a part in each slot, press RUN, and our simulator (the same one you build in the course) measures the goals. Dots on the wires show the current: the faster they move, the more current flows. Circuit tasks can be viewed as a schematic, as an isometric drawing, or built on a 3D breadboard you can turn around.
